 SWORN  a hook can quote one price and charge another
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  A v4 hook is code inside the swap, and it can tell a quote from a trade:
  tx.gasprice is 0 under eth_call, non-zero in a transaction. So it can
  promise one number and pay out a smaller one.

  Sworn re-quotes every route inside the transaction that settles, and
  reverts if what executed differs from what it probed.

  PART 1  Can a hook do this at all?             fixture, local EVM
  PART 2  Does it happen for real on Base?       live hook, forked
  PART 3  How often, and who is exposed?         the measurement
